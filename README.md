A newspaper front page that sums up the world news of Thursday 26 November 2026 in one image. The lead story is Kim Jong Un ordering a wider deployment of tactical nuclear weapons; the page also covers the Houthi missiles aimed at Riyadh that Saudi defences shot down, the US proposal of new tariffs on 14 trading partners, the Bank of Korea lifting its rate to 3.25%, and Musk promising two SpaceX shares for every child in America — with briefs on the Thanksgiving nor'easter and the 18th anniversary of the 26/11 Mumbai attacks. The image is drawn programmatically (Python PIL, DejaVu fonts) so every headline stays crisp and readable; `main.py` simply copies it to the output directory.
